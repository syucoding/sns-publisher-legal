SNS Content Publisher

A macOS desktop app that lets a solo creator publish their own content to their own social media accounts.

What it does

SNS Content Publisher runs on the creator's own Mac. It is used to draft and review a post, then publish the attached video or image to the creator's own account on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or Threads.

  • Write and review a draft, with its caption and per-platform settings.
  • Publish a video or image to the connected accounts the user selects.
  • Schedule a post to be published at a chosen time.
  • See the result of each publish attempt, including failures.

Everything runs on the user's own computer. There is no server operated by the app, no user registration, and no hosted service. The app talks only to the official APIs of the platforms the user has connected.

How TikTok is used

The account owner signs in once through TikTok's Login Kit to connect their own TikTok account. The app requests user.info.basic and user.info.profile so it can show the connected account's name and avatar on the settings screen, which is how the user confirms the right account is connected. It requests video.publish so that, when the user chooses to publish, the video file they selected is posted to their own TikTok account via Direct Post, together with the caption, privacy level, and comment, Duet, and Stitch settings they entered in the app. The app does not read or collect any other TikTok user's content, comments, or profile information, and it does not browse, search, or scrape TikTok.

Privacy at a glance

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain on the user's own machine.
  • No data is sent to the developer.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data sharing.
